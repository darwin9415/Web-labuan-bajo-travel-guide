À propos

Découvrez Komodo avec clarté, confort et sérénité

Komodo Season Trip helps you experience Labuan Bajo in a comfortable and well-organised way. We offer trips to Komodo National Park and the surrounding islands—from one-day and half-day sunset trips to private charters and city tours—with clear information from the start.

We designed this website to make it easy to explore trip options, understand the details and contact our team—without unnecessary steps. Planning your Komodo trip should feel clear and straightforward from the start.

For us, a good trip begins with clarity. Before you pay, you receive written information about the estimated schedule, facilities, excluded costs and safety conditions. We are transparent about weather, route changes and the possibility of seeing wildlife, so you can book with confidence.

Share your dates, group size and preferred style of travel. Our team will help you choose the right package, check availability and send a written offer through WhatsApp.

One-day route

Six destinations, one full day

The order of stops can be adjusted for weather, currents and tides, for your comfort and safety.

  1. 01

    Padar Island

    A short hike to a viewpoint that frames three sandy bays in a single view.

  2. 02

    Pink Beach

    A pink-tinged beach with clear water, ideal for swimming and relaxed snorkelling.

  3. 03

    Komodo Island

    Walk with a ranger to see Komodo dragons in their natural habitat.

  4. 04

    Manta Point

    Waters where manta rays often glide by. They are wild animals, so sightings cannot be guaranteed.

  5. 05

    Taka Makassar

    A white sandbar in the middle of the sea, surrounded by calm blue water.

  6. 06

    Siaba / Sebayur

    A calm snorkelling finale over the reef, with a chance of meeting sea turtles.

FAQ

Questions fréquentes

Which boat will I be on?

You choose the boat class. We confirm the name of the boat before departure and it always matches the class you booked.

Can I bring children or non-swimmers?

Yes. Tell us the child's age so we can prepare life jackets and a suitable snorkelling spot. Non-swimmers can join wearing a life jacket in safe waters, accompanied by the guide.

Will I definitely see Komodo dragons and other wildlife?

Komodo dragons live wild in their habitat. Guides and rangers know the best areas, but encounters with wildlife, including manta rays, turtles and flying foxes, cannot be promised.

What should I bring?

Reef-safe sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, a change of clothes, a towel, comfortable shoes for light trekking, and personal medication if needed.

How do I book?

Fill in the booking form or contact us on WhatsApp. We confirm availability, price and terms in writing before you pay.

Can the route change?

Yes. The route and order of stops may be changed, shortened, or swapped due to weather, currents, tides, or safety. Your guide will explain any changes on the day.

What are the cancellation and date-change terms?

Cancellation, date changes, and refunds may differ by package, date, and operating conditions. Please ask for the written terms via WhatsApp before you pay.

What if the weather is bad?

For safety, the trip may be adjusted, postponed, or cancelled. Date-change or refund options follow the terms we confirm in writing at booking.
